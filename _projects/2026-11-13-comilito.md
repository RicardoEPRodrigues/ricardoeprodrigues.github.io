**Comilito** is a desktop soundboard and mixer for Dungeons & Dragons Dungeon Masters. I built it to have a single place to prepare the sounds of a campaign and play them live at the table without fumbling between browser tabs and music players.

## How it works

Sounds are organised into three categories:

- **Music**: background tracks, usually one at a time, with crossfades.
- **Ambience**: looping layers such as rain, tavern chatter or wind, which can be stacked.
- **Effects**: short one-shot sounds such as a door slam, a dragon's roar or a spell being cast.

These are combined into **scenes**. A scene might be a music playlist, a few ambience layers and a set of effects for the pad. During a session you stack scenes in what Comilito calls the **Tapestry**. Higher scenes play over the ones below them: they duck the ambience underneath, and the top-most scene with music decides what plays. A "Combat" scene dropped on top of "Rainy night" brings in the drums and pushes the rain into the background. Taking it off brings the rain back. Scenes can have hotkeys, and whole stacks can be saved as presets and reused.

## Features

- One project per campaign, saved automatically as a plain folder with automatic backups.
- Projects can be exported and imported as a single `.comilito` file, with or without the audio.
- Loudness normalisation, so quiet and loud files play at a similar level.
- A searchable library with folders, tags, favourites and smart lists.
- Audio downloads from YouTube and other sites (through yt-dlp), or from direct links, optionally only part of a long video.
- Cropping sounds on their waveform.
- Themes, including your own and the desktop's accent colour.

## Tech

Comilito is a [Tauri 2](https://tauri.app) app. The audio engine and file handling are written in **Rust** using [kira](https://github.com/tesselode/kira), and the interface is written in **Svelte** and TypeScript. It runs on Linux and Windows, with macOS and Android planned.

Comilito was built with the help of AI (Claude, through Claude Code), with me directing the design and the decisions.
